## Post text

I stopped asking my AI team "what are you working on?"

So I built a page that answers it, live.

Building software with AI agents in parallel has a hidden cost: you lose sight of the work. Who is working right now? Whose turn is it, mine or the assistant's? What is blocked? When does the next release ship?

So I built SalesDay Mission Control. One page, always live:

- how many AI agents and agentic sessions are working right now
- the ball: what sits in my court and what sits in the assistant's
- progressing, pending, blocked, never touched
- the release in the gate and its tentative finish time
- load and memory of the build machine, as graphs that really move

The numbers are real: 443 tasks done, 53 open, 13 agents at work as I write this.

It is public on purpose. E-mail addresses, links, phone numbers and keys are masked before anything leaves my machine. No login. No dependencies. Open source.

Swipe for the tour. The live page and the code are in the first comment.

One question for you: if you could see a single number about your team's work at a glance, what would it be?
